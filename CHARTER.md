# Charter

See also: [Further Aligning W3C's Program of Technical Work with Human Rights](https://www.w3.org/TR/furthering-human-rights/)

This group focuses on impacts on society of the design of web technologies themselves, including when web technologies are misused. We’re looking both at threats & potential harms to society as well as examples of how web technologies support human rights. As discussed in the AB position paper, we are aligned with the UN OHCHR report, and therefore with the UN Declaration of Human Rights.

What do we mean by opportunities? We mean both opportunities for mitigations (of threats) **and** opportunities for positive use of web technologies to advance human rights goals.

Our aim is not to block or slow standards work, but to give groups a practical way to ask "who could this feature harm, and how" alongside the questions they already ask about interoperability, security and other areas of horizontal review.

## Goals

* Bringing in representation from NGOs and other organizations that have a focus on human rights topics in the digital domain, and actively survey them.   
* Make it easier for experts from civil society orgs and academia focused on societal impact of technology to share thoughts on ongoing work  
* Contribute to Societal Impact Questionnaire, principles, checklists, and other review documents that can be used for human rights due diligence / wide review.  
* Identify potential threats and harms to human rights on Internet and Web  
* Identify mitigation strategies and inform w3c work that improves our overall posture on human rights   
* reporting on human rights topics and the impacts of W3C standards  
* Organize breakout(s) at W3C events such as TPAC  
* Liaise with human rights focused groups at other technology standards organisations  
* Keep track of positive and negative societal impacts of W3C’s technical programme, including impacts that fall between or across existing horizontal review areas  
* Being a forum to discuss and examine the trade-offs that are inherent in these discussions and to identify areas where a nuanced approach is important

Non-goals: 

* We don’t seek to duplicate efforts with other W3C groups such as the Privacy wg, Sustainability ig, etc…

## Deliverables

* Wide review documents \- such as inputting into the Societal Impacts Questionnaire or potentially other documents  
	* Issue: how much force can such a document have when it’s not mandatory?  
* We will specifically work with the TAG to help produce the Societal Impacts Questionnaire  
* Surveys that bring concerns about the societal impact of the web from NGOs and other organizations to W3C  
* A framework for what a yearly report on human rights from W3C could look like  
* Explore a ranking of harms that we could apply to our work
