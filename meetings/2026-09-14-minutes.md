## TPAC Workshop Proposal

* Dan: current proposal is:

> In August of this year, the AB published a position statement [Further Aligning W3C's Program of Technical Work with Human Rights](https://www.w3.org/TR/furthering-human-rights/). Subsequently, we have created a [Community Group](https://www.w3.org/community/threats-and-harms/) where we are starting to explore how to put some of this recommendations into action. This will be a working session to present the AB position paper as well as the current status and activities of that group, and to gather community feedback, raise awareness of this topic and engage additional contributors.
