## Name of this group

Hidde: “Threats & harms” are negative impacts. W3C work also has positive impacts on human rights. For that reason, we are considering potentially changing this group's name to “Societal Impact CG”.

Dan: first of, tooling (Google Meet / Cryptpad etc) we use here is open for discussion, if anyone wants to change tooling, let us know!

Dan: in the [position paper on human rights](https://www.w3.org/TR/furthering-human-rights/) we suggested a community group where we could more easily bring in external organisations that are not members of W3C. Original idea was to call that a Human Rights CG, and we received feedback that this sounds too generic and unspecific. I came up with threats & harms as I've seen the phrase used in other efforts. At that point we did not consider looking wider than threats and harms. I think it would be good to scope it back to societal impacts or human rights factors. The [Societal Impacts Questionnaire](https://www.w3.org/TR/societal-impact-questionnaire/) at TAG was meant to be a self-questionnaire rather than official part of TAG design reviews. We are in contact with Lola and Sarven about contributing to this work

Tzviya: I think it would be good to talk to Sarven about the future of the societal impacts questionnaire, and potentially François and Philippe. I think some in W3C would see it becoming required as opposed to optional.  We don't want to create multiple new horizontal aspects of horgizontal review.  For this one we have to find a way to ... brains a bit more.

Simone: I like threats & harms... "if something is good it's not my problem".  Question: why social and societal?  

Jen: Social leans too much into social media... socital is about the larger picture. Any way we inpact the place in which we live.

Dan: pertaining to society.   E.g. privacy can have a relationship to power imbalance... which is a societal function.

Alex: Social is all connections between individuals... but Society is about groups and frameworks ... how things connects. Soeietal is more explanatory. Not just about the impacts on peoiple. We think about how the web ... impact on nature, and animals, can be related to sustaiability & society.

Hidde; I think societal is more about the system than the individual - not making the system bad for people.

Beckett: I agree - societal gives a face to things we usually take for granted in design.

Dan: just wanted to reflect how the above discussion should inform our scope. And the other work we are involved in like Web Sustainability Guidelines, WCAG, i18n… these are all aspects on how the web creates equity and improves society. We don't want to redo that work or step on toes of the people doing that work. It was exactly this thinking that went in to naming the Societal Impact Questionnaire. To Tzviya's point, that's why we chose to make it a self-questionnaire. Eg help people find out… have you thought about how your work could affect power imbalances… at the same time we should keep a scope that's doable and reference others where apt.
